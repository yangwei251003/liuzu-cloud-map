# Product

## Register

product

## Platform

web

## Users

Primary users are tourists planning a visit, exploring Six Patriarch Town remotely, or navigating the scenic area on a phone while walking. They need fast orientation, trustworthy place information, accessible controls, weak-network fallbacks, and clear separation between verified navigation and demonstration-only content.

Secondary users are professional guides and scenic-area operators who prepare routes, cultural explanations, map layers, facility records, review evidence, and publishable digital assets.

## Product Purpose

Build a responsive AI scenic-tourism platform around real geography, cultural evidence, practical itinerary planning, immersive cloud touring, and an always-available companion guide. Success means a visitor can understand the destination, create or reuse a route, inspect the map, receive honest guidance, save a journey, and ask the companion for help without confusing unverified information with official facts.

## Positioning

A trustworthy digital scenic-area companion that joins a detailed visitor map, evidence-grounded AI guidance, cultural storytelling, personal routes, and a lively local guide in one continuous experience.

## Brand Personality

Calm, warm, and trustworthy. The interface should feel rooted in Lingnan landscape and Chan culture while remaining contemporary, practical, and easy to use outdoors. The companion character adds liveliness and care without turning safety-critical screens into entertainment.

## Anti-references

- Generic tourism portals made from repeated promotional cards.
- Decorative Chinese-style interfaces that use texture, ink, or gold without a clear information hierarchy.
- Fantasy maps that alter real geometry or imply unverified navigation accuracy.
- Purple AI dashboards, glass-heavy surfaces, neon effects, and excessive gradients.
- Childish mascot overlays that block map controls or cannot be dismissed.
- Interfaces that hide uncertainty, data provenance, offline limits, or permission boundaries.

## Design Principles

1. Real geography and visitor safety take priority over visual fantasy.
2. The map is a working surface; video and illustration create emotion around it, not instead of it.
3. Every journey should move naturally from discovery to planning, guidance, memory, and sharing.
4. 禅小荔 behaves as a helpful companion with visible boundaries, not an omniscient authority.
5. One system serves phone and desktop through structural adaptation, not a scaled desktop layout.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG 2.2 AA for contrast, focus, semantics, keyboard use, text scaling, and touch targets. Support reduced motion, global mute, high contrast, simplified visuals, standard-map mode, browser zoom to 200%, safe-area insets, screen readers, and weak-network or offline fallbacks. Safety actions and official-help guidance must remain visible and usable without relying on color, animation, voice, or precise positioning.
